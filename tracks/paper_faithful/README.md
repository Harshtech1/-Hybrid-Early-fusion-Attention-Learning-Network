# Paper-faithful track

This track is reserved for reproducing the methodology described in the HEALNet NeurIPS 2024 paper.

It is intentionally blocked until the exact Kather100K-pretrained ResNet50 checkpoint and the authors' physical-resolution preprocessing policy are identified from authoritative provenance. No ImageNet weights may be substituted in this track, and native OpenSlide level 1 must not be represented as an exact 1.0 micrometre-per-pixel input.

No feature extraction or HEALNet training is authorized while `provenance/status.yaml` remains `BLOCKED_PENDING_AUTHOR`.
